History of Darbar e Hussaini

Darbar e Hussaini is a religious and cultural center located in the heart of the city. It was established in the year 2000 by the late Haji Abdul Ghani Khan, a well-known and respected figure in the community. The center is dedicated to promoting the teachings of Islam and providing a space for the community to come together and worship. The center offers a variety of programs and services, including daily prayers, religious education classes, and community events.
