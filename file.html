History of Darbar e Hussaini

Darbar e Hussaini is a religious and cultural center located in Lundo Sharif. The center is dedicated to promoting the teachings of Islam and providing a space for the community to come together and worship.

PROGRAM & EVENTS

Annual Program & Events

Darbar-E-Hussaini hosts major annual religious events including URS Mubarak, Jashan-E-Eid Milad-Un-Nabi (S.A.W), and Shahadat-E-Hazrat Ghazi Abbas Alamdar (A.S). Devotees gather for spiritual discourses, Naat assemblies, and community meals.
